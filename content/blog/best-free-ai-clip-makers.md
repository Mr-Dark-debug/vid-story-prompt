The best free AI clip maker is the one that lets you test the **complete workflow**—source import, candidate review, caption correction, crop, and export—before spending money. In 2026, “free” commonly comes with watermarks, credit or video caps, short project retention, lower resolution, or disabled editing. It is usually an evaluation tier, not a permanent production system.

Official pages were checked on **2026-07-31**. This is a documentation-based comparison, not a hands-on accuracy test. Recheck live terms before uploading because free allowances change quickly.

| Tool | Free allowance shown | Export/edit constraints shown |

These are not equivalent units. OpusClip uses processing credits; Vizard explicitly maps a credit to an uploaded minute on its pricing page; Submagic uses video count plus short duration/file caps; Descript combines media hours and AI credits across paid plans and exposes current Free details in its interactive comparison.

Do not convert these into “clips per month” without testing. A tool might generate ten candidates from one upload while only two survive context and caption review.

## OpusClip Free: useful long-source trial, constrained finishing

OpusClip's official page showed 60 credits monthly, 1080p rendering, auto reframe, AI captions, a watermark, no editing, and a three-day exportability window. Its help confirms 60 monthly processing minutes and watermarks on Free/Trial.

Use it to judge candidate selection, initial crops, and caption draft quality. The no-editing limitation matters: if boundaries or words are wrong, the free output may demonstrate the problem without letting you finish it. Export evaluation files promptly and keep source timestamps.

## Vizard Free: editor access with low-resolution, temporary projects

Vizard showed 60 credits/month, a private workspace, one social account, AI-generated clips, 720p export, full video editor, and three-day storage. Its help separately confirms a watermark and project expiry.

This is useful when you need to evaluate both automatic candidates and manual repair. Test whether the editor can correct the actual failure modes in your source. The 720p/watermark/retention constraints make it unsuitable as a clean long-term master path.

Vizard's older help pages can contain stale allowances, so prefer the current pricing page and checked date rather than search snippets.

## Submagic Free: very short finishing sample

Submagic's page showed three videos per month, 200 MB and 1 minute 30 seconds maximum, a watermark, Starter templates, and free stock media. That makes it practical for testing caption styles on short selects. It is not a like-for-like test of a two-hour podcast workflow.

If long-video extraction matters, inspect the current Magic Clips add-on and paid terms separately. Test name correction, word timing, caption line breaks, and whether stock/B-roll licensing fits the intended use.

## Descript Free: test transcript editing in the broader suite

Descript's Free tier gives a way to try text-based editing and AI tools, but the pricing page's detailed limits are interactive and can change. Confirm current media hours, AI credits, watermark/export resolution, and feature access in the live plan table.

The value of the trial is different: determine whether transcript correction, structural edits, filler handling, captions, and clip creation fit together for your team. Do not infer unlimited clip generation from the presence of a Free plan.

## The seven costs hidden by “$0”

### 1. Watermark

A watermark can be acceptable for evaluation and unacceptable for publication or client delivery. Never crop or conceal it in violation of terms. Price the first clean-export tier.

### 2. Reruns

Credits may be consumed by source processing, not successful approved outputs. Test what happens when transcription fails, you change a prompt, or you re-upload a corrected source.

### 3. Correction time

Count manual changes to words, boundaries, crop, and style. A free generator that needs a second paid editor is not a one-tool free workflow.

### 4. Project expiry

Three-day retention creates operational risk. Save source timestamps, transcripts where allowed, edit decisions, and evaluation exports. Do not keep the only approved project in a temporary workspace.

### 5. Resolution and format

720p or watermarked 1080p can prove a workflow but fail a delivery spec. Check frame rate, clean MP4, aspect ratios, caption files, alpha/audio needs, and NLE handoff.

### 6. Seats and brand control

Free tiers rarely model agency approvals, multiple clients, custom fonts, shared templates, and isolated workspaces. Test paid collaboration before committing.

### 7. Privacy and data use

No price does not mean no data cost. Review retention, model training, subprocessors, deletion, connector scopes, and terms. Never test confidential footage before authorization and security review.

## A 45-minute evaluation protocol

Use one non-sensitive representative source and a written acceptance sheet.

1. Record source size/duration and import time.
2. Save the tool's candidates and source timestamps.
3. Reject clips missing setup, qualification, or ending.
4. Correct names, numbers, negations, and speaker labels.
5. Watch the crop across every speaker/visual change.
6. Apply one simple brand/caption style.
7. Export the allowed file and inspect it on a phone.
8. Record watermark, resolution, expiry, consumed credits, and manual minutes.

Score approved candidates, not generated candidates. Repeat only with finalists; free credits are scarce and repeated exploratory uploads can obscure the result.

## When to upgrade

Upgrade when the test proves value and a paid tier removes a documented blocker: watermark, editing, storage, higher source allowance, export quality, seats, brand controls, API, or publishing. Do not upgrade to “make AI better” unless the tier explicitly changes the relevant capability.

Calculate total cost from source hours, reruns, seats, add-ons, storage, and reviewer time. Read cancellation, downgrade, and project-retention terms before building a library.
