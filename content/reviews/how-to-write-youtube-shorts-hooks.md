## What already passes

- **Intent and directness:** The first paragraph defines the full opening package rather than treating a hook as one clever sentence. The reader gets a usable cold-viewer test immediately.
- **Original usefulness:** The orientation–tension–proof-path contract, four synchronized opening parts and two-gate analytics diagnosis are genuine working methods. They improve on ranking pages that mostly offer interchangeable lines or universal thresholds.
- **Official metric alignment:** [YouTube's current Shorts Content-tab guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en) defines **How many chose to view** as the percentage of times viewers watched rather than swiped away. The newer [content-performance reference](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281) also describes **Stayed to watch** as the percentage that stayed past the opening. The article's diagnostic use is valid even though UI wording can vary across current Help surfaces.
- **Retention claims:** [YouTube's audience-retention documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415) says data normally takes one to two days, describes spikes as increased watching, rewatching or sharing, and dips as skipping or abandonment. It also says Shorts duration metrics are calculated from engaged views. The article reports these conditions accurately.
- **View-count change:** [YouTube's current Shorts overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070/get-started-with-youtube-shorts?hl=en-GB) confirms that from 31 March 2025 a view counts when a Short starts or replays with no minimum watch-time requirement, while the prior measure remains as Engaged views. The draft correctly keeps raw views and engaged viewing separate.
- **No fabricated benchmark:** The vidIQ result currently promotes a 70% or higher viewed-versus-swiped benchmark, but YouTube publishes no universal success threshold. The article correctly refuses to turn a vendor/community number into a platform rule.
- **Product truth:** Moment discovery, prompt search, complete-thought detection, standalone clarity, hook strength, transcript editing, caption correction and timeline rearrangement are Available in the canonical feature module. The draft treats scores as shortlist signals and does not promise virality. It does not imply subject tracking or multi-speaker layout availability.
- **Naturalness:** Examples are specific to tutorials, demonstrations, opinions, stories and podcast extracts. The prose varies its cadence, places limitations beside benefits and avoids generic AI filler, fake experience and inflated claims.
- **Corpus fit:** Article 15 owns opening design and hook diagnosis. Article 16 owns visual reframing; article 17 owns podcast moment selection. The contextual cross-links acknowledge those boundaries instead of restating either workflow.
- **Metadata:** The content parser reports 1,872 body words and the correct ten-minute reading time. Title, description, intent, summaries, FAQs, author, dates and related slugs validate.
