## Publication decision

The selection, editing, framing and caption workflow is strong, but the publication section needs one current YouTube-link correction before this article can pass. It tells creators to add the full episode's source URL to a Short description and cites YouTube's Video Clips documentation. That documentation is describing the separate 16:9 Video Clips output. [YouTube's current link policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639) says ordinary URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are non-clickable.

The source URL can remain in the description for attribution and copyability, but the article must say it is not a clickable doorway. It should use the native Related Video as the supported clickable route, add the official link-policy and Related Video sources to frontmatter/research, and keep the advanced-feature/public-or-unlisted conditions.

The article and FAQs state this accurately.

The article keeps the vertical edit as a separate step.

## Required revision

1. State explicitly that ordinary URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are non-clickable; a source URL can still serve attribution or be copied manually.
2. Describe Related Video as the clickable in-player bridge, available with advanced feature access and limited to public or unlisted channel content that follows Community Guidelines.
3. Add [Sharing links with your audiences](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639) and [Add a related video to your YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en) to the article sources and research note.
4. Re-run content validation, corpus audit and link checks.

The remainder of the article can pass without structural expansion once this publication-path correction is made.
