## Evidence and quality checks

- **Geometry:** The article accurately explains that filling a tall 9:16 canvas from a wide 16:9 source removes substantial width unless the editor uses a designed layout. It never recommends stretching the source.
- **YouTube display behavior:** [YouTube's current aspect-ratio guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6375112) says the player adapts to each video and advises creators not to bake padding or black bars into an ordinary upload. The draft preserves that rule while clearly identifying a full-frame image inside a deliberately designed vertical composition as an editorial treatment.
- **Shorts classification:** Current [YouTube Shorts upload guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12779649) supports square or vertical uploads up to three minutes. The FAQ and body do not turn 9:16 or 1080 by 1920 into the only accepted format.
- **Working resolution:** [Google Ads' current video specification](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13547298?hl=en) recommends 1080 by 1920 for vertical Full HD and 9:16 for vertical assets. The article uses this as a sensible export target, not as the organic Shorts rule.
- **Adobe Auto Reframe:** [Adobe's current sequence workflow](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/add-video-effects/commonly-used-effects/add-auto-reframe-effect-to-a-sequence.html) documents duplicate sequences, Slower/Default/Faster Motion presets and the need to refine keyframes for rapid movement or multiple points of interest. The draft's first-pass framing and cleanup advice is aligned with that source.
- **Competitor material:** Current CapCut, OpusClip and Descript documentation in the research note was used to understand auto-reframe, fit/fill, screenshare and multi-source layouts. The article describes these patterns generically and does not pretend to have run a hands-on product comparison.
- **Original usefulness:** The evidence map is the strongest contribution: it tells the editor to track the actual face, cursor, label, product, reaction or ball needed to support the narration before choosing a crop. The fixed crop, keyed pan, editorial cut, split layout and full-frame treatments then follow from that map.
- **Limits beside benefits:** Auto Reframe is paired with explicit failure checks. Full-frame preservation is paired with phone-size legibility. Moving crops are paired with jitter and late-arrival risks. Captions are placed only after composition stabilizes.
- **Product truth:** Moment selection, transcript editing, timeline rearrangement, caption correction and MP4 export are valid Available claims. Subject tracking and multi-speaker layouts are correctly stated as Coming soon.
- **Intent and naturalness:** The opening leads with the workflow and failure mode. Section shapes follow source types rather than a generic SEO template. The prose contains no fake test, performance claim, statistic or guarantee.
- **Corpus fit:** The article owns horizontal-to-vertical composition. Its links to the hook, podcast and dimensions guides provide adjacent detail without cannibalising their search intent.
- **Metadata:** The parser reports 1,785 body words and the correct nine-minute reading time. Frontmatter validates and the source set covers official, competitor and community evidence.
