## Key competitor facts

- Submagic is primarily a caption-first short-form video finishing editor.
- Free plan: 3 videos/month, 200 MB file limit, 1:30 maximum video duration, watermarked.
- Paid plans: Starter ($19/member/month for 15 videos, max 2 mins/video), Pro ($39/member/month), Business ($69/member/month).
- Magic Clips is presented as an add-on for long-video extraction.
- Alternatives:
  1. Vidrial: Transcript-first moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought verification, unmetered timeline editing.
  2. OpusClip: Built for long-video repurposing with virality scores and multi-speaker split layouts.
  3. Vizard: Automatic video clipping combined with a text-based manual editor.
  4. Descript: Comprehensive document-style script editor with Underlord AI and studio audio processing.
  5. quso.ai: Integrated video clipper and social media distribution calendar.

## Editorial angle

Submagic shines for polishing pre-cut short clips with flashy captions, but video-count caps and add-on long-video clipping make it less cost-effective for creators processing raw long-form recordings. Alternatives provide complete long-to-short repurposing pipelines with explicit transcript controls and flexible usage models.
