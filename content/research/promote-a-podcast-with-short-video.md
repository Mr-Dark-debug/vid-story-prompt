# Research notes: How to Promote a Podcast With TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts

Checked: 2026-07-31  
Backlog ID: 29  
Primary query: `podcast promotion clips`

## Intent and editorial boundary

The query asks for distribution strategy, not merely how to cut a podcast. This page owns campaign roles, platform adaptation, native paths to the episode, sequencing, and measurement. IDs 27 and 28 own caption design and audio-only visual production.

The original contribution is a three-role campaign map—discovery, proof, invitation—paired with a destination ledger. The measurement model separates clip response, next-step behaviour, and podcast outcome so “views” are not presented as proof of listen growth.

## Official platform findings

- Spotify for Creators advises choosing networks based on the show and experimenting with short video and audiograms. It supports experimentation, not a universal posting formula.
- Spotify's sharing help documents direct show/episode sharing. Availability and behaviour of links must still be checked per destination.
- YouTube's Related Video documentation says eligible creators with advanced feature access can attach a public or unlisted video from their channel to a Short.
- YouTube's link-sharing help says URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are non-clickable. This is why the draft explicitly rejects “click the description link.”
- YouTube's podcast setup help says full episodes belong in a podcast playlist and supporting Shorts should not be included in that playlist.
- Instagram Help provides current Reel upload aspect-ratio support. The draft uses vertical as a practical master and does not turn an upload specification into a ranking claim.

## Strong result inspection

Choppity and other ranking guides emphasize extracting hooks, captioning, and cross-platform publishing. Spotify's own guide covers network selection and format experimentation. The results frequently prescribe counts or imply virality without showing evidence. This draft avoids both and adds role-based selection plus destination measurement.

## Community evidence

The cited r/podcasting thread contains contradictory personal views about timing and platform results. That contradiction is useful: it supports treating timing and frequency as experiments. It is not used to state factual platform behaviour.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- No universal posting frequency, clip count, best length, or time of day.
- No guarantee that a clip will go viral or convert listeners.
- No cross-platform comparison of raw view counts as equivalent metrics.
- No claim that TikTok or Instagram link features exist for every account or region.
