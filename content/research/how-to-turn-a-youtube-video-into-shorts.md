# Research note: How to Turn a YouTube Video Into Shorts

- Backlog ID: 11
- Primary keyword: `youtube video to shorts`
- Checked: 2026-07-31
- Dominant intent: step-by-step conversion of an existing YouTube video into one or more Shorts, with a decision between YouTube's built-in path and an external editor
- Reader: a YouTube creator repurposing a video they own or are authorised to edit

## Search-result pattern

The official YouTube result answers only one narrow path: on mobile, a creator can open their own public long-form upload, choose Remix and Edit into a Short, select up to 60 seconds, then add creative elements. That resulting Short links back to the original. The option is unavailable for private or unlisted videos and videos with third-party copyright claims, and the source must allow Shorts sampling.

Many ranking pages flatten this into "paste any YouTube link into a downloader or AI tool." That omits rights, source quality, and the distinction between a platform remix and a newly exported Short. The broader official upload rule allows eligible square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes, while the built-in Edit into a Short selection remains up to 60 seconds. Those are different routes and must not be presented as a contradiction.

## Questions the article must answer

- Can a creator make a Short directly from an existing YouTube upload?
- Why might Edit into a Short be missing?
- When should a creator use the built-in YouTube flow or an external editor?
- What source should an external editor use?
- How do you choose a moment, repair its context, and frame it vertically?
- What current duration and format rules matter?
- How can a Short link viewers back to the original video?
- What rights and Content ID failures should be checked before upload?

## Gaps to beat

- Built-in and external workflows are mixed together.
- Public availability is treated as download permission.
- Guides repeat the old 60-second platform limit without explaining the current three-minute upload classification.
- The Short is framed as a crop rather than a standalone edit with context, caption, framing, and sound checks.
- Tutorials miss the Related Video option and the fact that ordinary URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments are not clickable.

## Non-commodity contribution

Build a **two-route decision tree**:

- Use YouTube's built-in Edit into a Short when the source is the creator's public upload, a single moment of up to 60 seconds works, and a direct attribution link back to the original is useful.
- Use the original source file or an authorised external-editor import when the clip needs more than 60 seconds, several moments, transcript-guided selection, precise caption correction, composed vertical framing, or a reusable master export.

The article adds a boundary-first edit method and a troubleshooting table for missing controls, lost context, poor crops, caption errors, rights claims, and non-clickable links.
