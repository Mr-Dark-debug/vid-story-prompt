# Research note: How to Repurpose the Same Video Across TikTok, Reels, Shorts and LinkedIn

- Backlog ID: 45
- Primary query: `repurpose video social media`
- Checked: 2026-07-31
- Dominant intent: Reuse one video efficiently without producing low-quality identical uploads or losing platform-specific context.
- Intended reader: A creator, marketing team, social editor, or agency coordinating several channels.

## Search and result review

Queries reviewed included `repurpose video social media`, current TikTok/Reels/Shorts upload documentation, `LinkedIn video specifications organic`, and `LinkedIn full screen vertical video`. Many guides offer a checklist of channels but omit source traceability, rights differences, clean-master preservation, and destination-specific calls to action. This draft treats repurposing as a controlled variant system.

## Official and primary sources

1. [TikTok Help: Creator tools and TikTok Studio](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-tools-on-tiktok?lang=nl) - current browser upload requirements.
2. [Instagram Help: Reel length](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/225190788256708) - current editor length and recommendation caveat.
3. [Instagram Help: Reel aspect ratio and resolution](https://www.facebook.com/help/1038071743007909) - accepted canvas and quality minimums.
4. [YouTube Help: Understand three-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en-EN) - classification and Content ID restriction.
5. [YouTube Help: Sharing links with audiences](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en) - non-clickable URLs in Shorts descriptions/comments and supported alternative link surfaces.
6. [LinkedIn Help: Video sharing troubleshooting](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548372/video-sharing-troubleshooting?lang=en) - current general upload size, duration, aspect, frame-rate, and edge-safe guidance.
7. [LinkedIn Help: Full-screen vertical video](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6828545) - device-dependent crop behavior and vertical player context.

## Result weaknesses addressed

- The shared master is separated from the final platform post.
- The article covers source/rights records, checksums or immutable paths, and corrections.
- Calls to action account for YouTube's current Shorts link behavior.
- LinkedIn feed and vertical-player crops receive a separate check.
- Results are interpreted with creative and platform context.
