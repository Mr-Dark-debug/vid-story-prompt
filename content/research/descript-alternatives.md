## Key competitor facts

- Descript is a full-featured text-based NLE and audio processor.
- Free plan: 60 media minutes/month, watermarked exports, basic AI Underlord access.
- Hobbyist plan: $16/user/month (annual) or $24 monthly, 10 media hours, 400 AI credits, 1080p watermark-free export.
- Creator plan: $24/user/month (annual) or $35 monthly, 30 media hours, 800 AI credits, 4K watermark-free export.
- Non-deterministic AI credit costs for certain Underlord actions can make usage unpredictable.
- Alternatives:
  1. Vidrial: Focused explainable moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought verification, zero complex track setups.
  2. OpusClip: Fast automated long-to-short clipping with virality scoring and multi-speaker split layouts.
  3. Vizard: Web-based automatic clipper paired with a light text editor.
  4. quso.ai: Clip extraction combined with social calendar publishing.
  5. Submagic: Fast short-form caption styling and social finishing.

## Vidrial product truth

- Moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought detection, transcript editing, timeline rearrangement, MP4 export: **Available**.
- Animated caption presets, long silence removal: **Beta**.
- Filler word removal, custom fonts, multi-speaker layouts, XML export: **Coming soon**.
- Free plan: 60 source minutes/month, 3 active projects, 2 GB storage, 720p, watermark (1 trial export clean).
- Paid plans: Creator ($18/mo) 600 mins, Pro ($39/mo) 1,800 mins.

## Editorial angle

Descript is a heavy, desktop-oriented multitrack editing environment. Creators who find Descript over-engineered or complex for simple short-form repurposing want lightweight web tools that specialize in moment discovery (Vidrial), automated clipping (OpusClip), or social distribution (quso.ai).
