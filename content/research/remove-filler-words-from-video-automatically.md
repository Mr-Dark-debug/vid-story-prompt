Checked: 2026-07-31

## Intent and page ownership

The reader wants a fast transcript-based edit without unnatural results. This page owns candidate detection, contextual decisions, bulk-action safety, audio/picture repair, caption refresh, and version comparison. It clearly distinguishes correction from deletion.

The original contribution is the “review queue, not mandate” model and a three-pass review: low-risk audio-only joins, visible-speaker edits, then high-context language.

## Official competitor research

- Adobe Premiere's January 2026 Text-Based Editing documentation describes detecting and bulk-deleting filler words and pauses, then refining the result on the timeline.
- Descript Help documents detection, individual actions, gap replacement, and its current Avoid harsh cuts option.
- Riverside Help documents filler-word recognition and editor removal, including that some disfluencies may not be recognized as standard filler words.

These sources confirm available competitor mechanics but do not prove every detected word should be removed. The article fills that editorial gap.

## Community evidence
