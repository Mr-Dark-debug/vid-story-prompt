# Research note: How to Turn Podcasts Into TikTok Clips

- Backlog ID: 42
- Primary query: `podcast to tiktok`
- Checked: 2026-07-31
- Dominant intent: Extract, frame, caption, and publish a coherent TikTok from a long podcast.
- Intended reader: A podcaster, producer, social editor, or agency managing guest content.

## Search and result review

Queries reviewed included `podcast to TikTok`, `TikTok Studio browser upload requirements`, `TikTok vertical video resolution official`, and `TikTok safe zone official`. Product-led results often stop after auto-clipping and captions. The draft adds rights, meaning preservation, two-speaker choices, music restrictions, live-preview checks, and series-level review.

## Official and primary sources

1. [TikTok Help: Creator tools and TikTok Studio](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/creator-tools-on-tiktok?lang=nl) - current browser upload file, resolution, size, and duration details; availability caveat retained.
2. [TikTok for Business: Creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en) - 9:16, 720p-or-higher, audio, and UI-safe creative guidance for ads.
3. [TikTok for Business: In-feed ad specifications](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en-GB) - current vertical recommendation and variable safe-zone factors.
4. [TikTok for Business: TopView specifications](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-reservation-topview?lang=en-GB) - safe-zone variation by stage, language, and device plus preview-tool advice.

Ad specifications are identified as ad documentation and are not presented as universal organic upload limits.

## Result weaknesses addressed

- Selection requires premise, claim, support, and finish.
- Organic upload and advertising documentation are distinguished.
- The article covers guest/media rights and commercial music uncertainty.
- It avoids claiming multi-speaker auto-layout availability.
- It measures understanding and payoff completion, not only views.
