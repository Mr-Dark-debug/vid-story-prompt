# Research note: How to Turn Long Videos Into Instagram Reels

- Backlog ID: 41
- Primary query: `long video to reels`
- Checked: 2026-07-31
- Dominant intent: Convert a long interview, podcast, webinar, or tutorial into a vertical Instagram Reel that is coherent and upload-ready.
- Intended reader: A creator, marketer, podcast editor, or social video producer.

## Search and result review

Queries reviewed included `long video to reels`, `Instagram Reels length official`, `Instagram Reel aspect ratio resolution official`, and `boost Instagram Reel requirements`. Ranking pages often collapse upload limits, recommendation behavior, and ad eligibility into one number. The draft separates them and puts story selection before cropping.

## Official and primary sources

1. [Instagram Help: Reel length and recommendation eligibility](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/225190788256708) - up to 20 minutes in the current editor and the over-three-minute recommendation caveat.
2. [Instagram Help: Reel aspect ratio and resolution](https://www.facebook.com/help/1038071743007909) - accepted aspect-ratio range, minimum frame rate/resolution, cover guidance, and high-quality upload setting.
3. [Instagram Help: Boost an Instagram Reel](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/570215404599013) - under-90-second, full-screen 9:16, and creative eligibility rules for boosting.
4. [Meta for Business: Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) - vertical video, audio, safe-zone emphasis, and preview/checker context for paid delivery.

## Result weaknesses addressed

- Upload maximum, recommendation eligibility, and boost eligibility are kept separate.
- The article explains candidate rejection, not only highlight selection.
- It builds a landscape story cut before shot-by-shot vertical framing.
- It covers cover crops, audio rights, phone monitoring, and post-publish diagnosis.
- It does not invent a universal safe-zone pixel measurement.
