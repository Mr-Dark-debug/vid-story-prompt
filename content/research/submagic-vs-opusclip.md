Checked 2026-07-31. Backlog ID 55; query `submagic vs opusclip`.

OpusClip showed Free (60 credits/month, watermark, 1080p, no editing, 3-day exportability), Starter $15 monthly, and Pro $29 monthly or displayed annual equivalent. Units are not treated as equivalent.
