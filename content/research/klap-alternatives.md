## Key competitor facts

- Klap.app provides AI video clipping, auto-reframing (AI Reframe 2), virality scoring, and transcript editing.
- Free trial: Allows 1 free video evaluation, watermarked exports.
- Basic plan: ~$29/month, up to 100 clips/month, 45-minute maximum source video length limit.
- Pro plan: ~$39–$79/month, 300+ clips/month, bulk video processing.
- Key limits: Source video length caps per clip on lower tiers.
- Alternatives:
  1. Vidrial: Explainable moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought detection, transparent source minute accounting.
  2. OpusClip: Credit-based (1 credit = 1 min) video repurposing with multi-speaker split layouts.
  3. Vizard: Automatic highlight detection paired with a manual text editor.
  4. Descript: Document-style script editor with Underlord AI tools.
  5. quso.ai: Clipping combined with social media auto-publishing calendar.

## Vidrial product truth

- Moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought detection, transcript editing, timeline rearrangement, MP4 export: **Available**.
- Animated caption presets, long silence removal: **Beta**.
- Filler word removal, custom fonts, multi-speaker layouts, XML export: **Coming soon**.
- Free tier: $0, 60 source mins, 3 projects, 2 GB, 720p, watermark (1 trial export clean).
- Creator ($18/mo), Pro ($39/mo).

## Editorial angle

Klap offers a slick 1-video trial and AI Reframe 2, but its $29/month entry point with a 45-minute source length limit can feel restrictive for podcasters and webinar creators with longer recordings. Alternatives offer more transparent source-minute accounting (Vidrial), larger credit pools (OpusClip), or script-level post-production (Descript).
