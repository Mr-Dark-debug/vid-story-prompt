## Key competitor facts

- quso.ai (formerly vidyo.ai) pairs video clipping and captioning with a social media management calendar.
- Free plan: 75 credits/month, 720p render quality, 7-day project retention, basic clipping.
- Lite plan: ~$19–$29/month, 100–200 credits, 1080p exports, scheduling for up to 6 social platforms.
- Essential plan: ~$26–$39/month, 300–600 credits, filler/silence removal.
- Growth plan: ~$33–$49/month, 600–1,200 credits, brand kits, analytics.
- Alternatives:
  1. Vidrial: Transcript-first explainable moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought checks, unmetered timeline editing.
  2. OpusClip: AI clipping with virality scoring and multi-speaker split framing.
  3. Vizard: Automatic highlight extraction paired with a manual text editor.
  4. Descript: Document-style script editor with Underlord AI and multitrack audio control.
  5. Submagic: Fast short-form caption styling and social finishing.

## Vidrial product truth

- Moment discovery, prompt search, complete thought detection, transcript editing, timeline rearrangement, MP4 export: **Available**.
- Animated caption presets, long silence removal: **Beta**.
- Filler word removal, custom fonts, multi-speaker layouts, XML export: **Coming soon**.
- Free tier: $0, 60 source mins, 3 projects, 2 GB, 720p, watermark (1 trial export clean).
- Creator ($18/mo), Pro ($39/mo).

## Editorial angle

quso.ai tries to combine post-production clipping with a full social media management platform. Creators who already use dedicated schedulers (like Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later) or who prioritize transcript accuracy and clip boundaries over social scheduling look for alternatives focused purely on video repurposing and editorial precision.
