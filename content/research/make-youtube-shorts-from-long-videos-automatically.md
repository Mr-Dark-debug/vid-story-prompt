# Research note: How to Make YouTube Shorts From Long Videos Automatically

- Backlog ID: 12
- Primary keyword: `long youtube video to shorts`
- Checked: 2026-07-31
- Dominant intent: step-by-step workflow with a tool-choice decision between YouTube's native editor and an external AI clipper
- Reader: a creator or small content team with authorised long-form YouTube footage who wants repeatable Shorts without treating every generated clip as publishable

## Search-result pattern

Ranking pages usually describe one of two different workflows without naming the distinction. Some explain YouTube's mobile **Edit into a Short** action, which lets the owner select up to 60 seconds from an already-public upload and links the Short to the original. Others describe an external AI clipper that transcribes a source, proposes several excerpts, reframes them, adds captions, and exports separate files. Calling both “automatic” causes readers to expect the native tool to choose moments or an AI clipper to publish without review.

Many 2026 pages still repeat the old 60-second platform maximum. Current YouTube Help says new square or vertical uploads up to three minutes can be categorised as Shorts. The native Edit into a Short workflow still selects no more than 60 seconds from the existing upload. The article must keep those two limits separate.

## Questions the article must answer

- What can YouTube's native Edit into a Short workflow do?
- When is an external AI clipping workflow more useful?
- Can a tool select clips without a human watching the full source linearly?
- What context must be checked around an automatically selected moment?
- How should landscape footage, captions, sound, and export be reviewed?
- What changes for Shorts longer than one minute?
- Which parts can be batched safely, and which need approval per clip?

## Gaps to beat

- “Automatic” is presented as one capability instead of discovery, editing, export, upload, and scheduling.
- Native YouTube selection and third-party AI clipping limits are mixed together.
- Pages promise a fixed number of Shorts per source or “viral” selection without evidence.
- Most workflows skip qualifiers outside the proposed clip, crop failures in demonstrations, caption correction, music rights, and final playback.
- Product pages rarely state that YouTube's native workflow requires a public upload owned by the creator and excludes private, unlisted, and third-party-claimed videos.

## Non-commodity contribution

Use an **automation boundary map**. Divide the job into source permission, brief, candidate discovery, context acceptance, boundary repair, reframing, captions, delivery, and release. Mark each step as safe to automate, automate with a gate, or keep manually accountable. Include a worked “context envelope” that reviews the sentences and visuals before and after each AI suggestion.

## Sources reviewed

### Primary and official

- YouTube Help, [Create YouTube Shorts from your videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12836917). Reviewed 2026-07-31. Supports owner-only public uploads, a maximum 60-second native selection, link-back to the original, lack of Audio Library music in this workflow, and exclusions for private, unlisted, or third-party-claimed videos.
- YouTube Help, [Upload YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12779649). Reviewed 2026-07-31. Supports computer uploads up to three minutes with a square or vertical aspect ratio and selecting up to 15 files before completing draft details.
- YouTube Help, [Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877). Reviewed 2026-07-31. Supports current classification and the global block on Shorts over one minute with an active Content ID claim.
- YouTube Help, [YouTube recommended upload encoding settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171). Reviewed 2026-07-31. Supports MP4, H.264, AAC/Opus, and preserving the recorded frame rate.
- Descript Help, [Create clips from your content](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10119670449293-Create-clips-from-your-content). Reviewed 2026-07-31. Supports topic/goal/criteria-guided candidate generation and separately editable clip compositions.

## Source-to-claim notes

- The three-minute maximum applies to current square or vertical uploads, not the native Edit into a Short selection, which remains up to 60 seconds.
- A Short over one minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked globally; do not weaken this to a monetisation-only issue.
- Descript documents candidate generation and editability, not reliable identification of the “best” or “viral” moments.
- No source supports a universal clips-per-hour ratio, automatic publishing as the default, or skipping context review.
